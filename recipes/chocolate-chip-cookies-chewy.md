ingredients:
- 1 stick (1/2 cup) butter
- dark brown sugar (1/2 cup)
- granulated sugar (1/3 cup)
- all-purpose flour (1 1/3 cups)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 1 egg
- 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips
---

### Instructions

- Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
- Melt butter in sauce pan. Gently simmer for 1 min. Take off heat and stir in sugars.
- Combine flour, salt, baking powder, and baking soda in bowl and stir.
- Mix the egg and vanilla into the cooled butter and sugars.
- Combine dry and wet ingredients in bowl.
- Stir in chocolate chips. Refrigerate dough 15 minutes.
- Scoop 2 tbs large portions onto baking sheet.
- Bake for 12-13 minutes.

---

### Fieldnotes

Not the greatest cookies you will ever have, but they are solid cookies and easy to make.
I would rate them 4/5 stars.

My first time, I made a single batch which produced 15 cookies.

My second time, I doubled the recipe and made 34 cookies.

Both times, I baked each sheet almost exactly 12 minutes and got very consistent results.

I put the extra dough in the fridge while waiting for the first batches to cook, which resulted in later batches spreading out less because the dough was slightly chilled.

**2026-08-04 Third Time**

I reduced the chocolate chips from 1 1/3 cups down to 1 cup.

The dough was very sticky right after mixing, but chilling it for 15 minutes made it very easy to work with and resulted in very consistent cookies. So, I removed the note in the instructions about chilling the dough being optional.
