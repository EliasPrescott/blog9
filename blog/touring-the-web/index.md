Offpunk is an offline-first command-line browser primarily used for browsing Gemini-space, which is an alternate web that strives for simplicity.

https://offpunk.net/

One of the features that makes Offpunk unique is its tour list.

<figure>

> Unlike most browsers you may know, there are no tabs in Offpunk. Instead, Offpunk has the concept of "tour". (shortcut "t").

> Technically, the tour is a FIFO list of all the pages you want to visit.

</figure>

[Advanced browsing with "tour"](https://offpunk.net/tour.html) (offpunk.net)

This design means when you find interesting links on a page, you don't have to open them right away, you can add them to your tour list to browse later at your convenience. Later can mean in a few minutes, or a few months. Offpunk doesn't care.

The tour list in Offpunk is a special list, but it is possible to create other lists as you see fit. Each list is simply a Gemtext file containing all the links you put into it. Offpunk even has a command for opening the list file in your editor so you can quickly modify them.

This is where it gets good. I stopped using Offpunk because it isn't super practical for browsing the modern web (not a bad thing). But just because I stopped using Offpunk, that doesn't mean I have to stop using my tour list.

Since I have been writing in a daily Gemtext journal recently, I thought why not maintain a tour list of links manually next to my daily journal files? When I find an interesting blog post to read in the morning, two things typically happen: I write down notes on what I am learning from it, and I find interesting looking links in it that lead to other pages.

If I follow each of those links, then I will end up spending too long reading and I will waste time. So, my solution is to copy good-looking links to the bottom of my tour list. That keeps me from opening multiple tabs and wasting time. Once I am done reading the original page, I can choose to open the first link at the top of my tour list, or I can do something else. Then later on when I am taking a break, I can open up the tour list and I will have an interesting link to follow waiting for me.

This method also creates a higher bar for opening a link. Since I open a link at a different time from when I find it, I have two opportunities to reject a link. When I open my tour list to grab the first link, I can always delete the link instead of opening it if I changed my mind.
